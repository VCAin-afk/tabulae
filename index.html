Our Purpose

We believe garments outlive trend cycles. TABULAE exists to collect, document, and rehome pieces that deserve a second life — every item cataloged with full honesty, condition disclosed, and nothing hidden. Here, clothing is not disposable; it is archived, and then passed on.

How It Works

  • Auction posts open at 8:00 AM (PHT) — bidding runs a 3-day phase, closing at 10:30 P.M. PHT on day three.
  • Bids are placed in the comments or chat of each auction post.
  • Minimum bid increment is ₱50. Bid retractions are not allowed once placed.
  • Any bid within the final 2 minutes extends the deadline by 5 minutes, until bidding stops moving.
  • A bidder who commits to the shop's Best Buy-It-Now price before deadline may lock the item and win outright.
  • After deadline, the highest bidder is messaged first. Full payment is due before the shipping date — cutoffs are 6:00 P.M. of Wednesday (for Thursday shipping) and 6:00 P.M. of Sunday (for Monday shipping). Unsettled payments forfeit the win, and the item moves to the second-highest bidder.
  • Shipping days are Thursdays and Mondays. Shipping is capped at ₱250; winning totals of ₱800 or more ship free.

Transparency & Condition

  • Every listing shows detailed photos, measurements, condition notes, and authenticity indicators.
  • Washing status is always disclosed — washed or unwashed/straight from archive.
  • Vintage garments may deviate 1–2 cm from listed measurements; ask in chat for exact values before bidding.
  • All questions are entertained before bidding — ask everything before you commit.
  • Sales are final. Remedies are offered only for items materially different from their listing (wrong item, undisclosed damage), reported within 24 hours of delivery with photo evidence.

Shipping & Tracking

  • Every winner receives the courier receipt, tracking number, and courier details at dispatch.
  • We monitor each parcel and message updates until the item is confirmed delivered.
  • Multiple wins in the same cycle ship together at one combined fee.

Payments

  • GCash (primary) · Maya · GoTyme Bank transfer — confirmed via payment proof before shipment.
  • COD is not yet available. Required paperwork and courier arrangements are still in progress; COD will return once settled. Trust runs both directions — this keeps early transactions simple and secure.

Discipline

  • Fake bids, repeat retracts, or backing out after winning = permanent ban, announced publicly on our Facebook Page.
  • All bidding, negotiation, and payment happen inside the TABULAE Facebook Page only. Off-platform deals carry no protection and are ban-worthy.
  • Bans are permanent by default; one appeal may be submitted via Page message or aviancm3@gmail.com. Verdicts are final.
  • All transactions, shipping updates, drops, and promotions are posted on the Page — turn on notifications.
